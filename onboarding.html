What's inside

Five places, one set of numbers — what you log in one shows up in the others.

  • ⌂HomeNet worth and how it moved, this month against the plan, bills due, and a monthly review that checks the figures add up.
  • ☰Cash flowA budget, income after tax, spending imported from a bank or card statement (CSV or PDF), and a bills calendar.
  • ◆Net worthAccounts, shares, crypto and super with daily prices, and property with its loans, offsets and rent.
  • $TaxIncome tax, Medicare and super worked out for you; payslips checked against the plan; each year's return and documents.
  • ⇄PlanRent-or-buy scenarios with stamp duty and LMI, projections years out, goals, and retirement.
✓ No account and no server: what you enter is kept in this browser. It only leaves when you choose to — sync sends it encrypted with your passphrase to your own Dropbox or folder, price updates ask for ticker names (never amounts), and Ask Claude sends an anonymised summary with your own key. Export a backup from the menu at the top right to keep a copy or move to another device.
